Reviewed evidence summary, not the complete publication. Source: Keeping Pace with Wearables: A Living Umbrella Review of Systematic Reviews Evaluating the Accuracy of Consumer Wearable Technologies in Health Measurement. Authors/institution: Doherty C et al.. Year: 2024. Evidence and environment: Terrestrial consumer-device validation umbrella review; 24 systematic reviews. Source sections reviewed: Methods; Sections 3.4–3.9; Discussion and limitations. Findings and applicability limits: Wearable accuracy, heart rate, SpO2, steps, sleep duration and energy expenditure. Accuracy depends on the specific device, outcome, firmware, population, activity and reference instrument. Heterogeneity prevented an individual-study meta-analysis. A pooled estimate from other watches is not an error specification for Amazfit Active 2. Brand similarity does not validate a model, and terrestrial validation does not establish validity in microgravity or partial gravity. Total sleep duration, sleep efficiency, stages and perceived quality are distinct outcomes. A step count is not a validated cross-environment measure of exercise loading.
