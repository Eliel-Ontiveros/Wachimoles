Reviewed evidence summary, not the complete publication. Source: Smart Devices and Wearable Technologies to Detect and Monitor Mental Health Conditions and Stress: A Systematic Review. Authors/institution: Hickey BA et al.. Year: 2021. Evidence and environment: Systematic review of terrestrial smart-device mental-health/stress studies. Source sections reviewed: Abstract; stress subsection; Discussion and limitations. Findings and applicability limits: Wearable stress, physiological arousal, heart rate, heart-rate variability HRV, electrodermal activity and self-report. Studies used different signals and assessment methods; HRV was prominent in stress research. Average heart rate and HRV are not interchangeable. Research on these signals does not validate an arbitrary proprietary stress score. This review does not provide a score-to-diagnosis conversion for Amazfit Active 2 or establish anxiety severity in an astronaut. A device stress indicator, a daily mood questionnaire and a clinical assessment are separate kinds of information. Heterogeneous terrestrial studies cannot establish diagnostic accuracy during isolation or microgravity.
