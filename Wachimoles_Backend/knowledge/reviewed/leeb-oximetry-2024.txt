Reviewed evidence summary, not the complete publication. Source: The performance of 11 fingertip pulse oximeters during hypoxemia in healthy human participants with varied, quantified skin pigment. Authors/institution: Leeb G et al.. Year: 2024. Evidence and environment: Controlled terrestrial desaturation; 34 healthy participants; eleven fingertip devices. Source sections reviewed: Methods; Findings; Discussion and limitations. Findings and applicability limits: Pulse oximetry, SpO2, arterial oxygen saturation SaO2, skin pigmentation and measurement bias. Performance varied across tested fingertip oximeters, including differences associated with skin pigmentation. The reference comparison used arterial measurements. These results do not validate a wrist sensor, the Active 2, or use during spaceflight. A single watch estimate cannot establish a diagnosis or determine an oxygen prescription. Device type, reference method and measurement conditions must remain attached to accuracy statements. This experiment does not quantify every possible motion or perfusion artifact, and its findings must not be turned into a universal correction for watch readings.
