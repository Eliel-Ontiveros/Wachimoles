Reviewed evidence summary, not the complete publication. Source: Human Biomechanical and Cardiopulmonary Responses to Partial Gravity – A Systematic Review. Authors/institution: Richter C et al.. Year: 2017. Evidence and environment: Systematic review; 43 studies; Apollo evidence and terrestrial analogs; no long-term studies. Source sections reviewed: Eligibility; simulation-model classification; Results; limitations. Findings and applicability limits: Lunar gravity, Martian gravity, partial gravity, hypogravity, locomotion and mechanical loading. Acute biomechanical and cardiopulmonary responses differ across gravity conditions and simulation methods. Body-weight support, parabolic flight and planetary surface operations are not equivalent exposures. None of the included studies examined long-term effects. Predictions about chronic adaptation must remain identified as predictions. Equal step counts cannot be assumed to represent equal ground reaction forces, energy expenditure or exercise loads across Earth, Moon and Mars. This evidence cannot quantify an individual’s six-month muscle loss on Mars or supply a validated planetary exercise prescription.
