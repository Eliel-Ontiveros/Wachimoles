Reviewed evidence summary, not the complete publication. Source: Shoulder Injury Overview & Treatment Guidance. Authors/institution: NASA Office of the Chief Health & Medical Officer. Year: 2025. Evidence and environment: Official technical synthesis; Earth-based NBL EVA training and surveillance. Source sections reviewed: History of Shoulder Injuries in Astronauts; Design of the EMU Planar HUT. Findings and applicability limits: Shoulder pain, reduced movement, limited range of motion, overhead tasks, spacesuit fit. The brief discusses restrictions on normal shoulder mechanics associated with the extravehicular mobility unit hard upper torso, repetitive motion, tools, and inverted training positions. Training surveillance is distinct from observing an injury during flight. This material does not identify the cause of a swollen or painful shoulder after exercise in microgravity. General muscle deconditioning cannot by itself explain acute focal swelling or functional limitation. Only the historical and suit-mechanics material is summarized here; clinical treatment tables and medication guidance are excluded.
